Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Scuffed Guard ("Scuffed Guard," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when the bot is used in Discord servers or when users contact us through support channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Scuffed Guard, we may collect or process:

  • Discord user, guild, channel, role, and message IDs
  • Discord usernames, display names, avatars, and profile information
  • Command and application interaction information
  • Message content necessary for enabled features
  • Embeds, attachments, reactions, and referenced messages associated with bot features
  • Forms, suggestions, reminders, AFK status, notes, birthdays, raffles, timers, logs, event activity, and game activity
  • Guild configuration, custom prefixes, permissions, moderation settings, blacklists, and feature preferences
  • Voting information received from top.gg
  • Support requests, feedback, and direct-message communications

Scuffed Guard does not intentionally collect passwords or payment information.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and improve bot features
  • Execute Discord commands and application interactions
  • Maintain guild-specific settings and configurations
  • Store timers, reminders, suggestions, rankings, event records, moderation records, and other feature data
  • Detect abuse, spam, and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain blacklists and security protections
  • Process votes and provide associated rewards
  • Provide support and respond to requests
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce bot or server rules

3. Data Storage

Scuffed Guard uses PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and local SQLite storage for different features.

Stored data may include Discord IDs, guild configuration, moderation records, user-created content, feature activity, and bot-generated records. Temporary JSON and database files may also be used to maintain application state.

We store only information reasonably necessary for the requested feature or service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by Discord, database providers, hosting providers, top.gg, and infrastructure providers used to operate the bot. These parties process information only as necessary to provide their services.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, maintain service security, prevent abuse, or enforce our rules.

We may share relevant information with server administrators or moderators when necessary to operate moderation, logging, support, or guild-management features.

5. Message and Discord Data

Scuffed Guard requires Discord's message content intent and may process messages, reactions, attachments, embeds, member activity, and referenced messages. This processing is used to perform commands or feature-specific tasks, including counting, moderation, reminders, suggestions, event or game tracking, scraping supported bot responses, and automation.

We do not continuously retain every message unless a feature requires it. Feature data remains stored until it is deleted, removed through a command or administrator setting, or no longer required.

6. Third-Party Services

Scuffed Guard interacts with Discord and may receive voting events from top.gg. Discord and top.gg have their own privacy policies and process data according to their respective terms.

Scuffed Guard may create Discord webhooks or post log information in configured channels when server administrators enable relevant features.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only while it is needed to provide the bot or comply with legal obligations.

Server administrators may remove guild configuration or feature data using available commands or by contacting support. Removing the bot from a server does not necessarily remove data already stored for a feature. Users may request deletion of specific personal or guild data through the contact methods below.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect stored information. These measures cannot guarantee absolute security, and we cannot prevent every unauthorized access or system failure.

Server owners and administrators are responsible for limiting bot permissions and reviewing channels or webhooks used for logs and moderation.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold
  • Request correction or deletion of personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request information about information sharing
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent

Discord users can control server participation and available Discord privacy settings. Server administrators can disable or remove bot features and configuration.

Requests may be submitted to:

  • Email: admin@scuffedguard.xyz
  • Discord Support Server: https://discord.gg/R9DrymMnrz

We may need to verify identity or server authorization before completing a request.

10. Children's Privacy

Scuffed Guard is not directed at children under Discord's minimum age or the minimum age required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so it can be removed.

11. International Data Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. By using Scuffed Guard, you acknowledge that information may be transferred and processed in other jurisdictions.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as features or legal requirements change. Updates will be reflected in the published policy. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
